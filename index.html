What does the graph tell us?

  • A steeper line means a higher speed. More distance is covered in each second.
  • A horizontal line means the car was stopped. Time passed, but distance did not change.
  • A curve that gets steeper means the car was speeding up; a curve that flattens means it was slowing down.
  • Distance never goes down — the odometer only adds up ground covered.
  • A straight part of the line means uniform motion — equal distances in equal times. A curved part means non-uniform motion.
  • The green dashed line joins the start to the end of your trip. Its slope is your average speed: total distance ÷ total time.
